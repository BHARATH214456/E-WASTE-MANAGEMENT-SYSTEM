The E-Waste Crisis: Why Discarded Electronics Threaten Our Planet

E-waste is the world's fastest-growing solid waste stream. When electronics are thrown into common trash, toxic heavy metals leach into drinking aquifers, and $62 billion in critical precious metals are buried forever in landfills.

Why E-Waste Cannot Go into Regular Trash

Three fundamental scientific realities explaining why certified circular disposition is vital:

1. Toxic Heavy Metal Leaching

Circuit boards and screens contain Lead, Mercury, and Cadmium. Landfill leachate carries these carcinogenic neurotoxins directly into soil and drinking water aquifers.

2. Fire & Explosion Hazards

Discarded Lithium-ion batteries in phones, laptops, and power tools experience violent thermal runaway fires when compressed inside municipal garbage compactor trucks.

3. $62 Billion in Lost Minerals

Recycling 1 million smartphones yields 34 kg of gold, 350 kg of silver, and 16,000 kg of copper. Urban mining prevents catastrophic virgin mining and habitat loss.

1. Structure & Semantics

Constructs the semantic hierarchy using accessible landmarks (<header>, <nav>, <main>, <section>, <footer>), native form controls with ARIA validation, and scalable vector SVG illustrations.
